Breast Cancer Detection Tool

This tool uses a trained neural network to analyze tumor measurements and predict whether a tumor is likely Malignant (Cancerous) or Benign (Non-Cancerous).

🌍 Server:
ℹ️ Where do these numbers come from? Doctors take a small sample from the tumor (called a Fine Needle Aspirate) and examine the cells under a microscope. The measurements below describe the size, shape, and texture of those cells. Larger, more irregular cells tend to be cancerous.
